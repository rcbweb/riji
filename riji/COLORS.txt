==========================================================
  COLOUR NAMES YOU CAN USE   (put in the COLORS list in coloc.py)
==========================================================

Grouped by colour. In each group the brightest is first and they get
darker going down, so you can guess a shade without seeing it.
You may also use a hex code like  "#ff8800".

**Colorblind-Friendly Pairs:**
For publication-ready, colorblind-safe images (accessible to those with red-green colorblindness), pair a GREEN (like 'green' or 'lime') with a PURPLE (like 'magenta').

**Shortcodes:**
matplotlib also accepts standard single-letter shorthand: r (red), g (green), b (blue), c (cyan), m (magenta), y (yellow), k (black), w (white).
